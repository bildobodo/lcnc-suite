# Viewer palette — research (2026-09-28)

Two research passes for [viewer-palette-fest.ideen.md](viewer-palette-fest.ideen.md): the LinuxCNC
GUIs read from source, and professional CAM systems and CNC controls from vendor documentation.
Earlier research (CNCjs, UGS, Candle, bCNC, NC Viewer, standards): [operator-punkte.recherche.md](operator-punkte.recherche.md).

## A. LinuxCNC GUIs, from source (LinuxCNC 2.9.4 installed; qtpyvcp e328b8c, probe_basic 8fc1c41)

- **glcanon table** (`rs274/glcanon.py` 422–475), used by AXIS, gmoccapy/gremlin, gscreen and all
  QtVCP screens (QtDragon, QtDragon HD, Woodpecker, QtAxis): black background, feed `#FFFFFF`, arc
  feed `#FFFFFF`, rapid `#4C8080` (teal), selected line `#00FFFF` 3 px, soft-limit box `#FF0000`
  1 px, preview 1 px opaque (alpha only with the non-default "alpha-blend program").
- **No dashing anywhere:** `glLineStipple` is set but `GL_LINE_STIPPLE` is never enabled; rapids
  differ by colour only (and a show-rapids toggle).
- **Executed motion = a separate trail** (C `positionlogger`, 10 000 points), 3 px, blended, by
  motion type: feed `#BF4040` α .75, arc `#BF4080`, rapid `#4C8080` α .25, jog `#FFFF00` α .75,
  tool change `#FFA600`, probing `#A121F0`. The preview itself is never dimmed as it executes.
- **Themes never change the 3D colours:** gmoccapy's GTK theme chooser restyles widgets only;
  QtVCP screens change only the background (QtDragon `#646464` gradient). Only AXIS has a light
  variant, loaded by hand through X resources (`axis_light_background`: white background, feed
  black, rapid `#0000F0`, selection `#08246B`; trail feed `#C04040` and limits `#FF0000` unchanged).
- **qtpyvcp VTK backplot / probe_basic:** background `#202425` (probe_basic), rapid `#C82323`
  red, feed `#D2D2FF`, arc `#6E6EFF`, executed "breadcrumb" cyan `#00FFFF` 2.5 px at 50 %, machine
  bounds grey `#737373`, no selection highlight, no light variant.

### Patterns

1. **Rapid colour has no common hue.** Rapids are red in Sinumerik, NX, hyperMILL (G0) and CAMWorks [S], and in the ESPRIT/EDGECAM figures. They are yellow in Fusion, Mastercam [S] and the SURFCAM sim, and green on Haas. **The shared convention is a second, non-colour cue:** dashed or dotted rapids (Alphacam, ESPRIT, EDGECAM "styles", Haas Setting 4, NX DashedCenter), or rapids that can be turned off (TNC "Feed only", Haas Setting 4 OFF, Mastercam backplot, Fusion/PowerMill link toggles, Alphacam work-plane moves). For stacked lines the dash plus the hide toggle is what makes the difference, not the hue.
2. **Feed is cool and rapid is warm.** Feed is blue in most CAM systems (Fusion, NX light blue, Mastercam [S], CAMWorks [S], SURFCAM) and green on Sinumerik. Haas (green rapid, black feed) is the outlier.
3. **The number of move classes differs.** CAM systems use 4–12 classes (NX 12 slots, Fusion 5–7, VISI 4). Controls draw 2 (rapid/feed) or 1 (Fanuc). For dense inspection the documented advice is to hide leads and links, not to tell them apart by colour (Fusion, PowerMill).
4. **Colour usually encodes a second dimension: tool/operation, not move type.** This holds for Alphacam, EDGECAM, Mastercam colour loop, SolidCAM [S], SURFCAM, HSM, VERICUT and NCSIMUL. Analysis modes add continuous scales: feed rate (hyperMILL, PowerMill, VISI, HSM blue → red), sequence gradient red → blue (Mastercam MachSim, SURFCAM, HSM Flow) and axis reversal (red).
5. **No system found greys or dims the executed part of the path.** The documented approaches are:
   - (a) show only part of it: before, after or a tail (Fusion/HSM);
   - (b) draw a trace that accumulates and has an explicit reset (Sinumerik, TNC, Haas, ESPRIT "Clear Trace");
   - (c) show history as stock colour per tool (VERICUT, NCSIMUL, Alphacam part colour).
   The only grey path states are: PowerMill's *inactive toolpath* grey (other toolpaths, not executed motion), and SURFCAM's *not-yet-checked* grey, which turns green once checked clear. SURFCAM's case is a close match to a collision sweep's progress.
6. **Selection.** Selection is documented only as a configurable highlight (EDGECAM, SURFCAM) or "highlighted" (Fusion). SURFCAM is the one vendor with explicit rules for line against line:
   - switch to an **alternate highlight colour when the entity already has the highlight colour**;
   - **double the thickness** of selected lines.
   No vendor gives a selection width in px.
7. **Collision/gouge is red wherever it is documented:** TNC7 DCM, TNC rapid-in-material, HSM timeline marker, SURFCAM, VERICUT [S]. Excess stock is blue (HSM, TNC7 white → blue). Alphacam makes the clash colour configurable. PowerMill only says "a different colour". **No vendor documents a colour for travel/soft-limit violations.** The closest is SURFCAM's rotary-angle colouring, used to "identify limit overruns".
8. **Bounds are rarely drawn.** Found: TNC blank frame, PowerMill translucent rapid/plunge surfaces, Haas locator window. **No machine travel-envelope display was found.**
9. **Theme.** No vendor documents a toolpath palette that adapts to a light or dark theme. Two things are documented instead:
   - a user-configurable scheme and background (Alphacam .col/.coltp + gradient, EDGECAM, VISI, SURFCAM schemes, NX per operation, hyperMILL global G0/G1, Fanuc palette parameters);
   - fixed control palettes (Sinumerik, Haas, TNC).
   EDGECAM's warning that a graduated background disturbs entity colours shows that vendors treat toolpath colours as tuned against one chosen background.
10. **Stated reasons for the colours:**
   - rapids mark non-cutting, wasted motion (NX blog);
   - rapid through material is a warning and turns red (TNC);
   - a colour change "provides a better contrast" (Mastercam tutorial);
   - colour points to the parameter group to edit (Fusion KB [S]).
   No source states "red = danger" for rapids in general.
